Privacy, in plain language

Last updated 20 September 2026.

What stays on your device

Your dhikr counts, custom phrases, timer history, settings, and reminder preferences are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Zikr does not require an account.

Encrypted backups

Backups are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase you choose. There is no cloud sync in this version. Use encrypted export and import to move your history between devices. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics are off until you choose them, either from the prompt on the home page or the toggle in Settings. When on, Zikr sends a random identifier that your browser generated for itself, plus whether it visited the site, used the app after setup, and opened it from a home-screen install. Each of those is recorded at most once per UTC day per browser. We do not send your counts, phrases, timers, targets, backups, feedback, email address, page URLs or referrer, and no device fingerprint is collected or derived. The endpoint rejects a message carrying anything beyond those fields rather than storing the part it recognises.

The identifier is kept on your device, separately from your practice data, and is not included in backups. Records are stored privately using Netlify Blobs, kept for up to 90 days, and deleted by a nightly job. Only aggregate figures are shown, on a dashboard behind a password. Netlify processes request information including IP addresses to host the site and to limit abuse; those addresses are not part of what we store.

Turning analytics off stops any further reporting and deletes the identifier from your device. Records already received expire on the retention schedule above; resetting the app does not reach them. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured even when the toggle is on. Activity while you are offline is not queued and sent later, it is simply not counted. The result covers only participating browsers, and counts browsers rather than people, so it is a floor rather than a total.

Notifications

Web reminders are optional, require explicit permission, and only work on supported installed devices. A configured push provider receives the browser-generated push subscription needed to deliver them.

Feedback

If you send feedback, your message, optional rating, selected language, and optional reply email are stored in our private Netlify Forms inbox. Netlify also processes technical request information for delivery and spam prevention. Your dhikr history is never attached. Feedback requires an internet connection. Clearing local app data does not delete submitted feedback. For deletion requests, contact us through the support channel.

Your control

You can export an encrypted backup or permanently erase local data at any time from Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes local Zikr data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project support channel linked on the support page.
