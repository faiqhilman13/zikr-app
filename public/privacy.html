Privacy, in plain language

Last updated 24 September 2026.

What stays on your device

Your dhikr counts, custom phrases, timer history, settings, and reminder preferences are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Zikr does not require an account.

Encrypted backups

Backups are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase you choose. There is no cloud sync in this version. Use encrypted export and import to move your history between devices. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics are off until you choose them, either from the prompt on the home page or the toggle in Settings. When on, Zikr sends a random identifier that your browser generated for itself, plus whether it visited the site, used the app after setup, opened it from a home-screen install, had the daily reminder switched on, and was opened by tapping a reminder. Each of those is recorded at most once per UTC day per browser. We do not send your counts, phrases, timers, targets, streaks, reminder time, backups, feedback, email address, page URLs or referrer, and no device fingerprint is collected or derived. The endpoint rejects a message carrying anything beyond those fields rather than storing the part it recognises.

The identifier is kept on your device, separately from your practice data, and is not included in backups. Records are stored privately using Netlify Blobs, kept for up to 90 days, and deleted by a nightly job. Only aggregate figures are shown, on a dashboard behind a password. Netlify processes request information including IP addresses to host the site and to limit abuse; those addresses are not part of what we store.

Turning analytics off stops any further reporting and deletes the identifier from your device. Records already received expire on the retention schedule above; resetting the app does not reach them. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured even when the toggle is on. Activity while you are offline is not queued and sent later, it is simply not counted. The result covers only participating browsers, and counts browsers rather than people, so it is a floor rather than a total.

Daily reminders

The daily reminder is off until you switch it on in Settings. It needs your permission to show notifications, and works only when Zikr is installed on a device that supports web push.

Switching it on has your browser create a push subscription: an address at the push service run by your browser’s maker, such as Google, Apple or Mozilla, and the keys that encrypt messages sent to it. We store that subscription with the time you chose and your time zone, so the reminder arrives at that time where you are, and the date the last one was sent, so you never get two in a day. If the push service turns reminders away, we also store the date it last did and on how many days it has since the last delivery. Nothing about your practice is sent to us: no counts, targets, phrases or streaks. Each reminder carries only the date it is for, encrypted so that only your browser can read it, and your device words it from what is already there. The push service can see that a reminder was delivered, but not what it says.

These records are stored privately using Netlify Blobs, and our logs note only how many reminders went out, never a subscription. When you open the app, it sends the subscription again if a week has passed since it last did, and straight away if the time, your time zone or the subscription has changed, so our copy stays correct. As with analytics, Netlify processes IP addresses to host the site and to limit abuse, and those are not stored with the reminder.

Turning the reminder off, or erasing your data in Settings, deletes the record. Switching off notifications for Zikr in your browser or device settings deletes it the next time you open the app. Otherwise it is deleted when the push service reports the subscription gone, as it usually does at the next reminder after you uninstall the app, clear its site data, or turn the reminder off while offline, or once the push service has refused it on 30 days with no delivery in between.

Feedback

If you send feedback, your message, optional rating, selected language, and optional reply email are stored in our private Netlify Forms inbox. Netlify also processes technical request information for delivery and spam prevention. Your dhikr history is never attached. Feedback requires an internet connection. Clearing local app data does not delete submitted feedback. For deletion requests, contact us through the support channel.

Listening to nasheeds

The Listen tab plays hand-picked recordings from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is requested from YouTube, not even thumbnails, until you tap play. Once you do, your browser connects to YouTube directly and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that player. Zikr does not tell YouTube what you count, and does not record what you listen to.

Your control

You can export an encrypted backup or permanently erase local data at any time from Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes local Zikr data.
