Privacy, in plain language

Last updated 12 July 2026.

What stays on your device

Your dhikr counts, custom phrases, timer history, settings, and reminder preferences are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Zikr does not require an account.

Encrypted backups and optional sync

Backups are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase you choose. If a deployment enables private sync, only the encrypted backup is uploaded. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Analytics

Anonymous product analytics are off by default. If you opt in, basic events such as opening a section or recording a count may be sent to the configured analytics endpoint. They do not include your phrases, counts, backup contents, or identity.

Notifications

Web reminders are optional, require explicit permission, and only work on supported installed devices. A configured push provider receives the browser-generated push subscription needed to deliver them.

Your control

You can export an encrypted backup or permanently erase local data at any time from Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes local Zikr data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project support channel linked on the support page.
